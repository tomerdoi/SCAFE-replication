## Review evidence and its limits

The ICSE 2023 paper, Section III-D (pp.4-5), describes paired student review of
functions flagged by tools, expert checks of a negative sample, and independent
expert re-examination/adjudication of disagreements and provisional positives.
Its review universe is tool-alarm-selected; not every negative had expert review,
and unflagged functions were not exhaustively audited. Section VI (p.10) discloses
possible missed mechanisms. This is useful upstream review evidence independent
of our model development, not proof of correctness, universal contract safety,
per-row reviewer lineage or applicability of every current CSV field. The README's
native-zero wording is insufficient by itself. The legacy collection and
call.value-focused positives also limit modern-reentrancy generalization.
