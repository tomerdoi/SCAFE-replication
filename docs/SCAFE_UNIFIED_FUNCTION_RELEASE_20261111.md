## Delivered outcome

The 2,323 normalized function views and three current source-label derivatives are
now joined into one versioned inspection release. This resolves the fragmented
artifact handoff, not the unresolved category semantics or training gate. All 18
original delivery hashes and every selected parent manifest payload are verified.

claim, canonical unknown/mask fields, source_label_assertions, AI-assessment status,
source group constraints and explicit eligibility blockers. Metadata/claims are not
model text; consumers must project model_input under a separately admitted protocol.

The pure helper scripts/consolidate_function_labels.py fails on missing/duplicate or
foreign IDs, provenance mismatch, changed canonical annotation protocol, code-hash
mismatch, invalid observation masks, missing definitions and duplicate/conflicting
same-definition assertions. A changed model view is not silently rebound to an old
label: its explicit binding-match field becomes false. Distinct source definitions
remain distinct; the helper does not alter existing loader or training gates.
