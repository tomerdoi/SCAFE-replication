## Revision fence and implementation consequence

Seven of the ten assessment context inventories contain another source revision; four before-repair inventories include future repair context. Those inventories were designed for audit/evidence comparison. They are not model-input packets. Existing model strings remain function-only; this diagnostic found no actual cross-revision model-input leakage and ran no model. A naive future concatenation of all declared contexts would violate the intended source-version boundary.

Any context carrier must bind every span to the original row revision, or to an explicitly verified unchanged source hash at that revision. It must be selected by a declared label-blind caller/modifier/state/callee rule, independently qualify newly included source/context fragments for rights, provenance and reserved-family/clone eligibility, mask annotations/comments under a verified protocol, and keep assessment prose, labels and repair-role metadata outside inputs. Do not simply feed the41audit-selected spans to a model. The carrier and its sampling/group/budget protocol are not implemented or admitted by this diagnostic.

If bounded qualified context cannot support the retained task, declare a genuinely function-local alternative protocol and requalify class support. Do not silently reinterpret current contextual labels, manufacture negatives or generate examples to hide missing context. The function-only baseline, a context alternative, and context/truncation ablations must eventually share the same legitimate grouped real development cohorts. Supervision-quality, source-use rights, partial-label training admission, group support, splits and matched baselines remain required before model fitting. Independent final-label completion alone is not the exploratory blocker.
